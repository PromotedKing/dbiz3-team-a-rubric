Fixed clock: 2026-09-27 10:00; no random numbers. Dates are ISO (`YYYY-MM-DD`, `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`), UTF-8, LF, rows sorted by primary key; an empty cell is NULL; BOOLEAN is `1` / `0`.
